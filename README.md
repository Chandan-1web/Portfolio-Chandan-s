## ✨ Overview

This portfolio presents my background as a developer through a structured, single-page layout. It highlights who I am, what I've built, the skills I bring, and how to get in touch — all without unnecessary clutter.

## 🧩 Sections

- **Hero Introduction** – Quick intro and tagline
- **Profile Summary** – Brief overview of my background and focus areas
- **Skills** – Technologies and tools I work with
- **Experience** – Internships and professional work
- **Projects** – Highlighted work with details and links
- **Resume** – Downloadable PDF resume
- **Contact** – Ways to reach me (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub)

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML** – Semantic, structured markup
- **CSS** – Responsive layout and styling
- **JavaScript** – Interactivity and smooth navigation
